## 1. The three corrections — LEADS THE SUMMARY

**AWAITING `ceo`'S LIST AND ORDER.** Asked at 15:5xZ on 9 September. The order is `ceo`'s to set because
the corrections are the summary's opening and the board reads the first sentence hardest.

The candidates from 9 September's record, with what each would say in a board sentence and the evidence
behind it — offered so the list is a confirmation rather than a composition:

**A. A published capability figure was withdrawn and then restored.**
A measurement relayed from another session said one of the sites behind a published capability claim had
served a placeholder page, which would have weakened the floor under it. It was posted to the data trail
without being re-derived, and it was wrong: the tool's own form probe had navigated away to a sign-in page
and the census counted that. The figure never lost its support, and the second correction restored it.
*Board sentence:* a number we published was withdrawn in error and is restored; nothing the board was told
about capability has changed.
*Evidence:* the two corrections on #311's trail, 11:00Z and 11:05:38Z.

**B. Work believed unrecoverable was recovered.**
The tracker was believed to destroy the record of who worked a row when the row closes. It does not: the
event that applied the label is kept permanently, and 189 of 295 closed rows still name their claimant and
the window — including 77 of the 83 sitting behind a branch with no open pull request. This matters to the
board only through the branch clean-up scheduled for the 15th, which would have made the question
permanently unanswerable.
*Board sentence:* a clean-up scheduled for the 15th would have destroyed the record of who did what; that
record turns out to have been kept all along, and is now read rather than discarded.
*Evidence:* PR #730, and the attribution added to the stranded-branch record by `tracker-auditor`.
*Re-measure at 07:25:* `node scripts/ready-label-audit.mjs` prints both figures.

**C. A measurement was caught being wrong before it was published, by a second measurement.**
Reading that record the obvious way returned a truncated answer that described itself as complete — the
count of events came back narrowed by the same limit that narrowed the events, so nothing in the response
could reveal it. Eight rows read as having no record when they had one. It was caught only because the
same population had been measured a second way and the two disagreed by seven.
*Board sentence:* the discipline of measuring twice caught a wrong number before it reached anyone.
*Note:* this one is a process claim, not a product claim. **If the board's three are product-facing, this
is the one to drop** — it is the most interesting to engineers and the least consequential to them.
